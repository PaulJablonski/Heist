About This Game

Deepwake is a co-op action roguelite built like a metroidvania. Every run drops you into the drowned city of Veyra, where rooms rearrange themselves each time you die, but what you learn about the place never resets.

Find a grappling hook or a pressure-proof dive suit and whole sections of the map open up for good. Then watch the tide rise: the longer you stay in a run, the nastier things get, and the better your loot needs to be.

Items that stack into nonsense. More than 220 relics combine in ways the game never warns you about. Set fire to your own bullets, turn dodges into shockwaves, or summon a swarm of eels that follow you around.

A world that remembers. Rooms are reshuffled every run, but unlocked traversal abilities, shortcuts, and lore stay with you. Early bosses come back with new tricks once you can reach them from the other side.

The tide never waits. Difficulty climbs on a live timer. Rush for a fast clear or farm for power and risk drowning in elites.

Play together. Online co-op for 1 to 4 players, with 8 playable divers, each with a distinct kit and a secret fifth ability to uncover.
